§ 01 Color

Six neutrals and one accent. Ratios are measured against the ground and recalculated when the accent changes.

Three greys carry the depth

Elevation is the only spatial cue. A panel steps from #0F0F0F to #161616; anything nested inside it steps again to #1C1C1C. Every edge is the same 1px #262626 hairline, and nothing casts a shadow.

§ 02 Typography

Archivo for display, IBM Plex Sans for reading, IBM Plex Mono for anything the machine wrote.

Tight at the top, open at the bottom

Tracking tightens as size grows — −0.04em at 88px, neutral at 17px, then opens back out to +0.14em for uppercase mono labels. Body copy holds a 1.6 line height and stops at 65 characters.

§ 08 Compliance

Measured, not assumed. Switch the accent in the header and these numbers move with it.

What has to stay true

Text contrast

Primary text 16.68:1 and secondary text 5.55:1 against the ground. Secondary drops to 4.94:1 on elevation +2 — still AA, and the floor for where it is allowed to sit.

Accent on ground

10.47:1 as text or as a ring. As a fill it carries #0F0F0F ink at the same ratio, so the primary button passes in both directions.

Focus

A 2px accent outline at 3px offset on every interactive element, never removed — well past the 3:1 minimum for non-text indicators.

Grain

4.5% opacity, overlay blend, pointer-events: none. It sits above the whole canvas but never intercepts a click, and it is decorative, so it is hidden from assistive tech.

Motion

Transitions cap at 180ms and touch color only. prefers-reduced-motion collapses them to zero, and nothing on this page animates on load or on scroll.

Theme

Dark only, by decision rather than omission. Every color is painted explicitly so the page holds its own ground instead of borrowing the host's.
